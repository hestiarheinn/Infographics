How to
order in
Shopee?

Simple steps for online shopping.

By Frezel Jan D. Tejedor

1. Search Item

Every successful purchase begins with knowing what you want. Use the search bar to find the product.

+
💡 Tip Box!
Use filters like “Price Range” or “4 stars & up” to quickly find quality items.

2. Select Product

Found what you need? Click on the product to view variations and shipping details.

+
💡 Tip Box!
Check if the shop is “Preferred” or “Shopee Mall” for safer and more reliable purchases.

3. Add to Cart

Don't check out just yet! Add it to your cart to apply vouchers later.

+
💡 Tip Box!
Add items during flash sales to lock in discounted prices before they run out.

4. Checkout

Select your items, apply vouchers, and choose your payment method.

+
💡 Tip Box!
Try using Shopee vouchers and coins together—you can save more than you think!

5. Place Order

Confirm your payment details and wait for delivery!

+
💡 Tip Box!
After placing your order, turn on “Order Notifications” so you won’t miss updates.
