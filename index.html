order in
Shopee?

Simple steps for online shopping.

By Kathryn Vera A. Lozano &
Frezel Jan D. Tejedor

1. Search Item

Every successful purchase begins with knowing what you want. Use the search bar to find the product.

+
💡 Tip Box!
Use specific keywords like "Matte Black" instead of just "Black" to find better items.

2. Select Product

Found what you need? Click on the product to view variations and shipping details.

+
💡 Tip Box!
Always check the "Reviews" section to see real photos from other buyers.

3. Add to Cart

Don't check out just yet! Add it to your cart to apply vouchers later.

+
💡 Tip Box!
Look for "Free Shipping" vouchers on the main page before adding to cart.

4. Checkout

Select your items, apply vouchers, and choose your payment method.

+
💡 Tip Box!
Double-check your delivery address and phone number to avoid delays.

5. Place Order

Confirm your payment details and wait for delivery!

+
💡 Tip Box!
Keep your phone nearby on delivery day so the courier can contact you.
